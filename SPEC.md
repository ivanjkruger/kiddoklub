# SPEC — KiddoKlub

**One sentence:** premium soft-play party rentals for kids 1-5 in Doha, delivered to the door with full setup, sanitization, photographer, and pickup, sold to expat + Qatari family households who'd rather host at home than at a venue.

**Target market:** mothers 28-45 in Doha villa compounds (The Pearl, Lusail, West Bay, Al Waab, Abu Hamour, Al Gharrafa, Education City), planning a 1-5yo birthday party or playdate, who already considered Mondrian / Banana Island / Hilton kids clubs but would rather control the experience and the photos.

**Why now:** inventory is fully stocked at the Waseef warehouse in Doha, the brand has run 8-10 paid events in the last 12 months without any organized outreach, the local kids-party economy is fragmented (~50 informal vendors, no clear premium leader), and Nadine (the founder + brand face) has the trust + voice to win the segment. The operator (Ivan) runs a Claude Code + James AI stack already; KiddoKlub gets a scaled-down version of the Equipt OS pattern.

**Revenue model:** weekend-driven home rentals at QAR 1,400 / 2,200 / 3,800+ per event, ~2,200 average ticket, 60-70% gross margin after photographer + helper + sanitization supplies. 10 weekend slots/month base, target QAR 22-50K/month MRR by Day 90.

**Phase 1 deliverable (Day 30):** kiddoklubdoha.com live with Cal.com booking + Skipcash deposit chain, Supabase CRM seeded with past clients + first-line bookings, IG content engine producing 5 posts/wk, Dream-100 weekly outreach running, 10 paid weekend bookings booked.

**Hard constraints:**
- KiddoKlub trades under Nadine's name (separate entity from Equipt). No Equipt CR, no Equipt bank, no Equipt invoicing. Equipt warehouse + setup team are arms-length services billed per event.
- Ivan caps personal time at ~30 min/day. Everything else is automated or routed through James.
- Premium positioning. No public pricing on the top tier. No "kid-coded" visual language.
- Voice: warm, parent-to-parent, never corporate. EN + AR in parallel, never auto-translated.
- Banned content: em/en dashes, AI-tells, "elevate / transform / unleash" copy patterns.

**What "shipped" means for v1:**
- Real parent books a real party on the site
- Skipcash deposit clears
- Wati / WhatsApp confirmation lands
- James pings @Ivan_james_bot
- Setup team executes
- Post-event testimonial + Google review request fires automatically
- Booking shows in dashboard

**What's explicitly NOT in scope (for now):**
- Theme catalog (Phase 3)
- AR Lens for Snap (Phase 3)
- Hire helper (Phase 3 trigger: utilization >75%)
- Indoor venue partnerships (Phase 3 / summer)
- KSA / UAE expansion (year 2+)
- Voice cloning (Phase 3 backup only)
