# KiddoKlub Partners

Reciprocal-referral relationships. Each row tracks one human who can send us bookings or vice-versa. Live source-of-truth in Supabase `partners` table; this file is the human-readable layer for outreach planning.

## Partner types

### Photographers (highest leverage)
We give them: free portfolio access at our setups, mutual tagging.
They give us: shoots at our events (we keep usage rights), referrals when their clients ask "who set this up?"

### Event planners (Tier 1)
We give them: QAR 150 referral fee per booking + we make their party the IG-worthy one.
They give us: 2-4 bookings/month per active planner.

See full Dream-100 in `content/dream-100.md` (15 planners + 4 decorators).

### Cake makers
QAR 100 referral fee. Every cake order = a parent who needs entertainment.

### Nurseries + preschools
Free demo at end-of-term parent event = trojan horse to 50-200 families per nursery.

### Mom influencers (Tier 3)
Free rental in exchange for honest content + post. Pick 5-15K followers, NOT 100K+ (engagement collapses above that for kids services in Doha).
