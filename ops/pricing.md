# KiddoKlub Internal Pricing Notes

**Public source-of-truth:** `content/packages.yaml`. This file is internal margin + decision logic.

## Cost stack per Klub Classic event (QAR 2,200 ticket)

| Line | QAR |
|---|---|
| Equipt Qatar setup-team fee (per-event) | 250 |
| Equipt Qatar warehouse + transport allocation | 100 |
| Sanitization supplies | 30 |
| Photographer (when included) | 350 |
| Card fees (Skipcash 2.49%) | 55 |
| Wear-and-tear amortization (~3% of ticket) | 65 |
| **Total cost** | **850** |
| **Gross margin** | **1,350 (61%)** |

## Cost stack per Klub Signature event (QAR 3,800 base)
Photographer + themed decor + gift bags pull cost to ~QAR 1,400. Margin holds at ~63%.

## Why Signature has no public price
- Forces WhatsApp conversation, where conversion is 3-5x the web form
- Anchors Classic as "the affordable option" (Hormozi rule for QAR 5K+ B2B applies here too)
- Lets Nadine dynamic-price by season + venue (Pearl + Lusail can take +20% comfortably)

## Discount discipline
- Founding-family slot: free bouncy castle + free 90-min photographer on Classic. Capped at 10 bookings, expires 2026-06-30. NOT a price cut on the headline.
- Weekday 20% off (Sun-Thu) — only on Mini and Classic, NEVER on Signature
- Rush +15% (inside 7 days) — applies to ALL tiers, no exceptions
- Multi-event discount: 2nd booking within 90 days = 10% off; 3rd = 15%. Codes only, never published.

## Price increase triggers
- Weekly utilization >75% for 4 consecutive weeks → bump Classic + Signature 10-15%
- Founding-family slots filled → remove bouncy castle inclusion from Classic, becomes paid add-on
- After CR + insurance live + 25 reviews on GBP → bump Classic to QAR 2,400, Signature to QAR 4,200 base

## What we never do
- Match cheaper competitors. We're the premium operator. If a parent wants QAR 800, we're not the brand.
- Cash payments
- Same-day booking under 24 hours (operational risk too high)
- Outdoor June-August (heat liability)
- Bypassing deposit
