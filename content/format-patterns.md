# KiddoKlub Format Patterns — the structural layer

File 2 of 3 (Van Clief Constraint 05). Scopes structural decisions per channel: length, shape, sequence. Grows when a new channel is added. For voice see `voice-tone.md`; for prohibitions see `constraints.md`.

## Default structure (every channel)
- **BLUF:** lead with the one thing the reader needs to know.
- Short paragraphs, 2-3 lines max.
- Bullets only for lists of 3+.
- Sentences end in periods or question marks. Semicolons OK.

## Per-channel shape
| Channel | Shape |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp opener | Max 4 short lines, max 280 chars. No walls of text. |
| DM opener (IG) | Max 3 short lines. |
| Email subject | Max 50 chars, no clickbait. |
| IG caption | Max 220 chars above the "more" cut. |
| Reel hook | Max 8 seconds read aloud. |
| Cal.com confirm / Skipcash receipt | One calm confirming line + next step. No marketing. |

## First-touch shape (DM, IG, WA cold)
Specific observation about the recipient → curious question → stop. No pitch, no price, no link, no brand name. The pitch only appears after they reply.
- **Good:** "Hi Sara! Saw your daughter just turned two, those balloon photos were gorgeous. Quick question, did you set them up yourself or did someone do it for you?"

## Reactivation shape
Voice note, never text (Doha gets 4-5x reply rate on WA voice notes). Nadine records in her own voice, max 30 seconds, references the kid by name + the last package booked.

## Photo testimonial shape
Always ask AFTER delivering the photos, never before. "We just dropped 24 edited photos in your DM, would you mind leaving us a Google review when you have a sec? Here's the link."

## Sign-off shape
"Nadine x" or "Nadine 🤍" (one emoji max, only heart-cream or heart-bone). Never "— Nadine", never "Best, Nadine".

## Emoji placement
🌈 on hooks (one per line max) · 🤍 on sign-offs · 🎈 on add-ons · 🥳 on milestone celebrations. Never stack; never open a line with 🎉.

## Bilingual shape
EN and AR written in parallel by a native Arabic speaker. AR uses informal-warm register (حفلة not حفل). Typography: 29LT Bukra (Arabic) + Inter (Latin) + Fraunces (display). Never Tajawal.

## Where this applies
DM, WhatsApp, email, IG caption, FB post, Snap caption, reel hook, ad copy, web copy, Cal.com confirmation, Skipcash receipt. Anywhere a human reads us.
