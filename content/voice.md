# KiddoKlub Voice Guide

Read this before drafting anything outbound. Anything that fails the rules below gets rejected by `kiddoklub-voice-audit`.

## Core voice
KiddoKlub speaks in **Nadine's voice** — a Doha mom talking to another Doha mom. Warm, specific, calm. Never corporate, never salesy, never AI-coded.

If a line could appear in any kids-party brand's marketing, rewrite it until it could only be ours.

## Length + structure
- BLUF: lead with the one thing the reader needs to know
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Bullets only for lists of 3+
- Sentences end in periods or question marks. Semicolons OK. **Dashes never.**
- WhatsApp messages: max 4 short lines for the opener. No walls of text.

## Banned characters (auto-rejected)
- Em dash `—`
- En dash `–`
- Spaced hyphen used as dash ` - `
Use periods, commas, semicolons, parentheses instead.

## Banned phrases (AI tells in 2026 — instant reject)
- "elevate" / "transform" / "unleash" / "next-level" / "level up"
- "game-changer" / "unlock" / "premium experience" / "world-class"
- "we're excited to" / "delighted to" / "thrilled to"
- "let me tell you" / "here's the thing"
- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "delve" / "tapestry" / "navigate the landscape"
- "boost your" / "supercharge"
- Any line that starts with "🎉" or three emojis

## What to do instead
- "Creating smiles with play and party magic 🌈"
- "We bring the play, you take the photos."
- "Set up by 8am, packed down before bedtime."
- "Pastel rainbow palette, real Doha mums, real photos."
- "Your villa garden becomes the venue."

## Emoji rules (canonical, per Drive/.../Ops/_BRAND-RECONCILIATION-Session-B-2026-04-25.md)
- 🌈 on hooks (one per line max). 🤍 on sign-offs. 🎈 on add-ons. 🥳 on milestone celebrations.
- Never stack emojis. Never start a line with "🎉" or three emojis.
- Sign-off: "Nadine 🤍" or "Nadine 🌈". Never "— Nadine".

## Bilingual rule
EN and AR are written in **parallel** by a native Arabic speaker (or Ivan with Mohammed's review). Auto-translation is banned — Doha Arab moms spot it instantly. AR uses حفلة (informal "party"), not حفل (formal "event"). Use TheMix Arabic or 29LT Bukra typography, never default Tajawal.

## First-touch rule (DM, IG, WA cold)
First message is 100% relationship, 0% pitch. Never lead with "I run KiddoKlub" or "we offer". Never put a price, a link, or a brand name in the opener. Lead with a specific observation about the recipient + a curious question. Reveal the pitch only after they reply.

**Bad:** "Hi! I run KiddoKlub, we deliver soft play to your villa. We have a free castle this week, want to book?"

**Good:** "Hi Sara! Saw your daughter just turned two — those balloon photos from her party were gorgeous. Quick question, did you set them up yourself or did someone do it for you?"

## Reactivation rule
Voice notes, never text. Doha gets 4-5x reply rate on WA voice notes vs typed. Nadine records in her own voice, max 30 seconds, references the kid by name + the last package they booked.

## Photo testimonial rule
Always ask AFTER you deliver the photos, not before. "We just dropped 24 edited photos in your DM — would you mind leaving us a Google review when you have a sec? Here's the link."

## Sign-off
- Nadine: "Nadine x" or "Nadine 🤍" (one emoji max, only ever heart-bone or heart-cream)
- Never sign "— Nadine" (em dash banned). Never sign "Best, Nadine" (corporate).

## Tone calibration examples

| Situation | Bad | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | "We're delighted to confirm your booking!" | "All locked in for Saturday. We'll WhatsApp Friday with arrival time." |
| Quote follow-up | "Following up on your inquiry" | "Hey [name], wanted to make sure that quote landed in your inbox" |
| Reactivation | "It's been a while! Time to book again?" | (voice note) "Hi Sara, it's Nadine. Cannot believe Layla's almost 4. We'd love to do her birthday again if you're up for it." |
| Cold DM | "Hi! We deliver soft play in Doha." | "Hi Reem, that pastel setup you posted last weekend was insane. Did you style that yourself?" |

## Apply this to every channel
DM, WhatsApp, email, IG caption, FB post, Snap caption, Reel hook, ad copy, web copy, Cal.com confirmation page, Skipcash receipt note. Anywhere a human reads us, this voice runs.
