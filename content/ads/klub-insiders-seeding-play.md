# Klub Insiders — the mom-seeding play

*Channel #1. Gift free setups to ideal-profile Doha moms in exchange for authentic content + word-of-mouth. This is both our acquisition engine AND our content engine. Created 2026-06-09.*

## Why this is channel #1 (above planners)

- Solves our biggest gap: we have zero real content/proof. Seeding manufactures authentic UGC at real parties.
- The seeded mom's audience IS the ICP: same compounds, same-age kids, real local engagement.
- Doha mom word-of-mouth is the highest-trust channel that exists for this buyer.
- Cost is marginal, not cash: ~QAR 600-900 per seed (helper + sanitisation + transport; we own the inventory), not the QAR 2,200 ticket.

Planners become channel #2; activate them once we have proof to show them.

## The one rule: ICP-audience fit beats follower count

A real 3K-follower Pearl mum with a 3-year-old and genuine local comments beats a 50K "influencer" with a bought national audience. Every time. Audience match is the whole game.

## Dream Moms — selection criteria (score each, gift the top 3-5 first)

A candidate qualifies only if MOST of these are true:
- **Kid 1-5** — has a child in the active birthday/playdate window
- **Target compound** — Pearl, Lusail, West Bay, Al Waab, Abu Hamour, Al Gharrafa, Education City (or clearly Doha-based)
- **Local audience** — followers are real Doha moms, not bought or national/regional. Comments come from local accounts.
- **2K-50K followers** — big enough to matter, small enough to be authentic and reachable
- **Already shares family/party content** — birthdays, playdates, kids activities; posts naturally
- **Aesthetic fit** — clean, warm content that will represent the brand well
- **Reachable** — open DMs, posts in the last 2 weeks

Disqualifiers: bought followers, no young kids, mega/regional influencer, obvious giveaway-hunter, off-brand chaos.

## The offer (light, never contractual)

**She gets:** a free Klub Classic setup for her kid's next birthday or playdate. Full service: delivery, setup, sanitisation, pickup. Optional theme of her choice.

**We ask (warm, not a contract):**
- Tag @kiddoklub in 2-3 stories during the party
- One feed post or reel after
- Let us repost her content (we always ask first)
- A short voice or text note on how it went

Photo permission stays hers. We ask before we ever repost.

**Cap:** 3-5 seeds in the founding window (to 2026-06-30). Measure inbound per seeded mom, then decide whether to extend. After the seed phase it is a paid product, never an open freebie treadmill.

## The IG DM sequence (relationship-first; 0% pitch until step 3)

Rules: James drafts, Tier B approval, **Nadine sends** from her account; max 10 IG DMs/day; follow + genuinely engage for 3 days before step 1; never lead with the brand or the offer. AR drafts below are starting points and MUST be native-validated (informal Gulf register, حفلة not حفل) before sending; route every message through `kiddoklub-voice-audit` + dash-audit.

**Step 0 — Warm up (days 1-3):** follow her, like and leave one genuine comment on a recent kid/family post. No DM yet.

**Step 1 — Opener (pure relationship, no brand, no offer):**
- EN: "Hi! I found your page through the Doha mums world; your little one is the sweetest 🤍 I am a Doha mum too, based in [compound]. Are you nearby?"
- AR (native-validate): "هاي! لقيت حسابچ من عالم أمهات الدوحة؛ بنتچ/ولدچ حلو وايد 🤍 أنا بعد أم بالدوحة، ساكنة بـ[المنطقة]. إنتي قريبة؟"

**Step 2 — Build rapport (after she replies):** keep it human, find the hook (an upcoming birthday, a recent playdate, where she hosts). Still no pitch.

**Step 3 — The seeding invite (only once there is real warmth):**
- EN: "Quick thing; I actually run KiddoKlub, we do soft-play setups at home for the little ones. I would love to set one up for [kid's name]'s next party as a gift, no charge. I love how you share your days and I would be thrilled if you posted a few photos if you love it. Zero pressure either way."
- AR (native-validate): "شي بسيط؛ أنا عندي KiddoKlub، نسوي ركن ألعاب ناعمة بالبيت للصغار. أحب أجهزلچ وحدة لحفلة [اسم الطفل] الجاية هدية مني، بدون أي مقابل. أحب طريقتچ بمشاركة يومچ وبكون مبسوطة لو نشرتي كم صورة إذا عجبچ. ولا يهمچ بالمرة."

**Step 4 — Logistics (after yes):** date, compound, kid count, theme choice; route to WhatsApp to lock it.
