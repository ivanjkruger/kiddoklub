05

Voice & tone.

Mom to mom; warm, exact, never cartoon. We write the way Nadine talks on WhatsApp; bottom line first, no marketing verbs, semicolons before dashes.

We sound like

  • Warm, not effusive; specific, not generic
  • Bottom line up front; the price, the date, the thing
  • Plain nouns: "soft play", "ball pit", "rug"; not "experience"
  • Semicolons for rhythm; commas for breath; periods for stop
  • One promise per sentence; no stacked adjectives
  • Confident about the standard; quiet about the brand

We never sound like

  • "Elevate", "transform", "unleash", "magical", "amazing"
  • "Memories that last a lifetime"; "where dreams come true"
  • Em or en dashes anywhere; semicolons or full stops only
  • Cartoon copy: "wow", "epic", emoji bursts, exclamation runs
  • Auto-translated Arabic; we write each language natively
  • Claims of being a registered company, LLC, CR, MOCI

06

Photography direction.

The hero is always the overhead flatlay before any child arrives; everything else is supporting. Action shots are second tier; never a hero, never a banner.

Composition

Camera straight down.

90 degrees overhead; lens centred over the rug; props arranged in a loose triangle, not a perfect grid.

Light

Natural, soft, from one side.

Sunlight from camera-left at 10 o'clock; no flash, no fill, no warm filter. The Doha morning gives us the light we want.

Crop

Four-by-five, calm corners.

Always 4:5 or 1:1; bottom-left corner left quiet for the wordmark; never crop a prop in half at the edge.

07

Motion & interaction.

Movement is small, slow, skippable. The hero parallaxes a touch; sections fade in once; everything respects reduced motion.

Soft play, set with care.
Hero parallax · 5% Y on bg · 1.04 scale on fg · scroll to test
Parallax

Hero only.

Background translates 5 percent of viewport on Y; foreground scales to 1.04. Nothing else parallaxes; never a card, never a thumbnail.

--parallax-y: 5%
--parallax-scale: 1.04
Reveal on scroll

Once, gently.

8px translateY plus opacity; 350ms ease; fires once when 18 percent visible. No staggered cascades, no delays.

duration: 350ms
distance: 8px
threshold: 0.18
Reduced motion

Respected, fully.

If prefers-reduced-motion is set, parallax stops, smooth-scroll stops, reveals jump to final state. No exceptions.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)

Honest scope · the no-CR line

What the brand is not.

KiddoKlub is operated informally; under Nadine personally. There is no CR (commercial registration), no LLC, no MOCI listing; the brand never claims any of these on a public surface.

11

Bilingual & RTL.

English and Arabic both lead; neither is a translation of the other. The site flips wholesale, not piece by piece.

Engineering rules
  • Direction at the root. dir="rtl" lang="ar" sits on the html element; never toggled per component.
  • Logical CSS only. All spacing uses inline-start, inline-end, block-start, block-end; no left or right anywhere in production CSS.
  • One typeface per language. 29LT Bukra for Arabic only; never Tajawal, never any system Arabic.
  • Numerals. Western numerals across both languages for prices and dates; consistency over local convention.
  • Punctuation. Arabic uses "،" and "؛"; Latin uses "," and ";". Em and en dashes banned in both.
قواعد ثنائية اللغة
  • كتابة أصيلة في كل لغة. النص العربي يُكتب بالعربية مباشرة؛ لا يُترجم آلياً.
  • خط واحد لكل لغة. 29LT Bukra للعربية فقط؛ نتجنّب تماماً خط تجوال.
  • الاتجاه. يُضبط dir="rtl" على عنصر html، وليس على كل مكوّن.
  • الأرقام. أرقام لاتينية للأسعار والتواريخ في اللغتين.
  • الترقيم. الفاصلة العربية "،" والفاصلة المنقوطة "؛"؛ ممنوع استخدام الشَّرطة الطويلة.

12

The don't list.

The fastest way to look off-brand. Each line below is a hard stop; no exceptions, no "just this once".

✕

Use any em (—) or en (–) dash anywhere in the system.

It is the surest tell of auto-generated copy; we use semicolons, commas, and periods only.
✕

Run an action shot of children as the hero image.

The hero is always the overhead flatlay before guests arrive; action shots are second tier.
✕

Auto-translate Arabic from English, or vice versa.

Every Arabic surface is composed natively, by an Arabic-first writer.
✕

Set Arabic in Tajawal, IBM Plex Arabic, or any system Arabic.

29LT Bukra exclusively; the licence sits in Nadine's name.
✕

Claim KiddoKlub is an LLC, a CR-registered company, or a MOCI-listed brand.

The operation is informal under Nadine personally; the brand never implies more.
✕

Reference any other business the founder is involved in, anywhere on a KiddoKlub surface.

The brand is operationally and visually independent.
✕

Quote Klub Signature pricing as a fixed figure on a public surface; always "starting from QAR 3,800".

The figure is the floor; bookings are quoted on date, theme, and compound.
✕

Use cartoon vocabulary: "magical", "amazing", "wow", "epic", "transform", "elevate", "unleash".

It belongs to other brands at the inflatables-and-foam end of the market.
✕

Saturate the palette into primary red, primary blue, primary yellow.

Color Pop stays dusty; pink is dusty pink, yellow is butter, never neon.
✕

Stamp the wordmark over a busy zone of a flatlay; or burst a row of emoji into copy.

Wordmark sits in a quiet corner; emoji at most one, only where it earns its place.
✕

Toggle direction with a "rtl" CSS class on individual elements.

Direction is set at the html root; layout uses logical properties only.
✕

Invent a fourth theme; "Boho Garden", "Safari", "Princess", "Jungle".

Three themes, locked: Neutral Nest, White Wonderland, Color Pop.
