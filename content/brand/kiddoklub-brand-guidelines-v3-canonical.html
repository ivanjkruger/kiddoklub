Brand Essence

A Doha mum's favourite vendor; quietly recommended at school pickup.

We are a real, warm, smiling kids' brand; bright pastels, rainbow signature, action-and-laughter photography, founded and run by a real mum. Same premium soft-play that a venue gives you, in your own home, with no driving, no crowd, no time pressure; and the prettiest setup the mums in your group chat have seen this year.

02

Logo system

Three pastel arcs, one rounded wordmark.

The logo is a pastel rainbow arc above a soft, rounded, lower-case "kiddoklub". Every letter alternates the brand pastels. The rainbow is our signature mark; we do not crop it off.

Clear-space

A full lower-case "k" of clear space on every side. Other elements never touch the rainbow or the wordmark.

Minimum size

The full mark sits at 32px height minimum on screen, 18mm in print. Below this we use the wordmark only, in a single brand colour.

On photography

On dark or busy photo backgrounds, drop the mark on a 92% cream rounded rectangle so the pastels stay legible. Never use a hard white container.

05

Voice

A friendly mum recommending her favourite vendor at school pickup.

We are warm, specific, useful. We use first names, we ask first, we send voice notes when text would feel cold. Emojis are welcome in moderation; one per body line, plus the brand 🌈 on hooks and the 🤍 on sign-offs.

Use

  • ✓Specific nouns: "white fencing", "ball-pit cushion", "balloon arch"; never vague "magical setup".
  • ✓BLUF: lead with the answer, then the texture.
  • ✓Semicolons to connect related thoughts.
  • ✓"We" when speaking as the brand. "I" when Nadine answers WhatsApp.
  • ✓One emoji per line. Brand 🌈 on hooks. 🤍 on sign-offs. 🎈 on add-ons.
  • ✓Voice notes for reactivation, not text. 4 to 5x reply rate in Doha.

Never

  • ✕Em or en dashes (— or –). Always the #1 AI tell. Use a semicolon or a full stop.
  • ✕Marketing verbs: elevate, transform, unleash, ignite, curate, amplify, level up, unlock, game-changer.
  • ✕"Premium experience", "world-class", "we're excited to", "delighted to".
  • ✕Multiple emojis in a row. One per line, never three.
  • ✕Auto-translated Arabic. Better English than broken Arabic.
  • ✕First message that pitches. Opener is 100% relationship, 0% pitch.

06

Photography

Action is the hero; setup is the supporting cast.

Real KiddoKlub reels and posts that win in this category lead with kids playing, parents reacting, balloons close-up, soft-play in motion; not strict overhead flatlays. Bright, warm, saturated. Cropped faces (under 5) for privacy; never blurred. The setup overhead is a beautiful B-roll, never the headline image.

DO
Do · action as hero
Kids playing. Bright daylight. Pastel ball-pit in frame. Faces cropped or oblique. The shot you would screenshot to your group chat.
DO
Do · sun-warm styling
Pastel rainbow palette. Real props. Bright and saturated; never desaturated to look "editorial".
DO
Do · close-up detail
Balloon arch, ball-pit cushion, ribbon detail. Warm depth-of-field. The texture sells the booking.
DON'T
Don't · primary clash
Saturated red, blue, yellow at full intensity. Reads as a kids' party shop. Never our look.
DON'T
Don't · desaturated muted
Filtered to look "editorial". Robs the joy out of the photo. We are not muted; we are warm.
DON'T
Don't · low-light empty
Setup before the curtains opened. No life, no light, no invitation. Bin the shot.

Editing preset 🌈

  • ✓Bright + warm + saturated. Lift exposure +0.3 to +0.5 stops; warm white-balance +200K; saturation +5 to +10.
  • ✓Skin tones gentle, never orange-pushed.
  • ✓Highlights protected (balloons should not blow out).
  • ✕Never desaturate. Never push contrast hard. Never apply ARRI-grain "cinematic".

Photo permissions

  • ✓Default: faces of children under 5 are not posted publicly without explicit per-event consent.
  • ✓Reaction shots (parents laughing, hands clapping, candle blowing) without face ID are always allowed.
  • ✓Setup overheads, props, balloon arches: free use, signature B-roll.
07

Motion & interaction

Bouncy, friendly, never decorative.

Motion in KiddoKlub is a smile, not a flourish. Hero parallax, reveal-on-scroll for storytelling beats, gentle bounce on hover, full respect for prefers-reduced-motion.

Hero parallax

5% Y travel and 1.04 scale across the first viewport scroll. Driven by Framer Motion useScroll; eased curve.

Reveal-on-scroll

Comparison rows, package cards, theme tiles fade in (8px Y, 0.35s) with a 40ms stagger. once: true; never animate twice.

Reduced-motion

All non-essential motion stops at @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). Hero parallax disables; reveals snap in. Functionality never depends on motion.

Theme system

Three themes, every one a smile.

Neutral Nest, White Wonderland, Color Pop. Each carries its own palette extension and props list; all three are bright, photographable, and rooted in the rainbow palette.

Theme · 01

Neutral Nest

Cream, butter, peach, soft sage. The default look; calm, warm, photographable on every villa floor. Carries a baby's first birthday and a quiet baby shower without rework.

Props: rattan crates, linen banners, dried pampas, wooden ABC blocks, cream balloon arch, white fencing, butter ribbons.

Theme · 02

White Wonderland

Soft white core; pearl, ivory, mint, and cream accents. The "photographs like a magazine" look; best in light-filled villas with high ceilings or beach mansions.

Props: pearl-finish soft-play, white fencing, white ball pit, mint accent balloons, silver foil number, ivory gauze ceiling drape.

Theme · 03

Color Pop

Mint, dusty pink, butter, sage; full rainbow palette in proportion. The "joyful and bright" look; ideal for second and third birthdays, full-rainbow themes, and Eid family parties.

Props: pastel ball pit (mint/pink/butter/sage mix), rainbow balloon arch in seasonal palette, gingham picnic cushion, paper rosettes, paper rainbow garlands.

11

Bilingual & RTL

EN and AR are equals; never one through the other.

Arabic is written natively, by a native speaker. Layout flips via Next.js layout-level dir="rtl" on the AR route only and CSS logical properties; never via class toggles, which break Framer Motion x-axis animation.

Engineering rules

  • ✓Two routes: / and /ar. Each has its own layout with the right lang and dir.
  • ✓Spacing uses margin-inline-start, padding-inline-end; never margin-left.
  • ✓Numbers, prices, phone numbers stay LTR inside RTL paragraphs (use <bdi> when needed).
  • ✓Toggle reads "العربية / EN" as text; never flag icons.
  • ✓Numerals are Western (0 to 9) in both languages.

Copy rules

  • ✕Never auto-translate. Better English than broken Arabic.
  • ✓Arabic uses 29LT Bukra; web fallback IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Tajawal acceptable when budget locked.
  • ✓Voice in Arabic mirrors voice in English: warm, BLUF, emojis allowed in moderation.
  • ✓Use حفلة (informal) not حفل (formal) for "party".

12 · The hard list

Don't.

  • No em dash (—). No en dash (–). Use a semicolon or a full stop.
  • No "elevate", "transform", "unleash", "level up", "game-changer", "world-class".
  • No primary clown palette. No saturated red, royal blue, school-bus yellow at full intensity.
  • No muted-only "editorial Anthropic" aesthetic. We are warm and saturated, not beige.
  • No action shot banned from the homepage. Action and reaction are the hero.
  • No exact Klub Signature price on a public surface. Always "starting from"; quote on WhatsApp.
  • No Equipt branding. No Equipt invoicing. No Equipt logo on a customer surface.
  • No CR number, no LLC, no MOCI; the operation is informal under Nadine personally.
  • No machine-translated Arabic. Never.
  • No mascot, balloon-shaped logo, or hand-lettered "playful" font that competes with the wordmark.
  • No multi-emoji string. One per line. Brand 🌈 on hooks, 🤍 on sign-offs, 🎈 on add-ons.
  • No flag icons in the language toggle. Text only ("العربية / EN").
