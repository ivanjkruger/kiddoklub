# KiddoKlub Brand — canonical short reference

*One page, the source of truth. Matches the live site (`app/globals.css`) and the logo. Supersedes the stale "Fraunces / terracotta" notes in CLAUDE.md. Long form: `kiddoklub-brand-guidelines-v3-canonical.html`. Created 2026-06-09.*

## Essence
A Doha mum's favourite vendor, quietly recommended at school pickup. Warm, soft, premium-but-friendly. Three pastel arcs, one rounded wordmark. The feeling is "calm, clean, joyful," never loud or kid-coded.

## Logo
- Lowercase **kiddoklub** wordmark + concentric pastel rainbow arch (mint, pink, butter).
- Files: `public/brand/logo-header.png` / `.webp`, `public/brand/logo.svg`, `content/brand/assets/logo.svg`.
- Clear space = the height of one "k". Never recolour, never add a drop shadow, never put it on a busy photo without a cream scrim.

NEVER primary red, blue, or green. Pastels on cream only.

## Type
- **Quicksand** — display / headings (rounded, matches the wordmark). Weight 600, tight letter-spacing.
- **Inter** — body, captions, UI.
- **29LT Bukra** (fallback IBM Plex Sans Arabic) — all Arabic surfaces.
- Rule: "Quicksand speaks. Inter explains. Bukra carries Arabic." Western digits 0-9 in both languages.

## Photography
- **Hero = overhead flatlay BEFORE kids arrive.** Calm, styled, the set as the star.
- Action / kid candids go to stories and reels, never the static hero.
- "Action is the hero; setup is the supporting cast" applies to MOTION; stills stay calm.
- We never fabricate kids or a set we did not deliver. Real footage only; AI may animate real stills.

## Voice (full docs: `content/voice.md`)
Warm parent-to-parent (Nadine), BLUF, semicolons not dashes, no AI tells. EN + AR as equals, AR never auto-translated. A friendly mum recommending her favourite vendor.

## Do / Don't
- DO: cream space, soft pastels, rounded shapes, real photos, gentle motion.
- DON'T: primary colours, pure black, serif display, drop shadows, clip-art, em/en dashes, "elevate / transform / unleash," kid-coded clutter.
