The look, the words, the feeling we leave behind.

01

Brand Essence

Parties your child will remember; setups your guests will photograph.

We are not a kids' party shop. We are a styling service for the most photographed hour of a mother's year. Everything we do, from font to fence to flatlay, is built to make that hour effortless for her and unforgettable for her child.

Who we serve

Mothers in Doha villas and the Pearl, Lusail, West Bay, Al Dafna compounds, planning a 1st through 5th birthday, a baby shower, or a milestone playdate.

What we sell

Premium soft-play, themed styling, and quiet logistics; delivered, set up, sanitized, and collected. The product is the photograph that comes after.

Why we win

Same premium soft-play a venue gives you, in your own home, on your own time, with no driving, no crowd, and the photo set already arranged.

02

Logo System

A serif wordmark, set with confidence.

The mark is typographic, not iconographic. It carries warmth from the italic, premium from the serif, and trust from the proportion. No mascots, no balloons, no fonts pretending to be handwritten.

KiddoKlub
KiddoKlub

Clear-space

A full lower-case "k" of clear space on every side. Never let other elements; nav links, photo edges, badges; touch the wordmark.

Minimum size

22px on screen, 14mm in print. Below this, the italic loses its grace and the kerning collapses.

Mono variants

Ink on Bone (default). Bone on Ink (dark surfaces). Single-colour Terracotta only on white photography overlays where Ink would compete with the image.

05

Voice

Warm, parent-to-parent; never cartoonish.

Nadine talks to mothers. She is one. Tone is calm, specific, and slightly understated; the opposite of the exclamation-heavy, emoji-loaded language most party services lean on.

Use

  • ✓Specific nouns: "white fencing", "ball-pit cushion", "balloon arch"; never "magical setup".
  • ✓BLUF: lead with the answer, then the texture.
  • ✓Semicolons to connect related thoughts.
  • ✓"We" when speaking as the brand. "I" when speaking as Nadine in WhatsApp.
  • ✓One quiet exclamation mark per testimonial; zero in body copy.

Never

  • ✕Em or en dashes (— or –). Always the #1 AI tell. Use a semicolon or a full stop.
  • ✕Marketing verbs: elevate, transform, unleash, ignite, curate, amplify.
  • ✕"Magical", "amazing", "the best", "out of this world".
  • ✕Multiple emoji in a row. One, occasionally, at the end of a WhatsApp message.
  • ✕Auto-translated Arabic. Better to send English than broken Arabic.

06

Photography

The hero is always the room before the children arrive.

Overhead, even light, no faces, props arranged. Action shots earn their place in the second-tier grid; never as the headline image. This rule is what keeps the brand from looking like every other party rental.

Do · flatlay hero
Overhead. Sunlight from camera-left. Soft-play arranged in the final shape; cushions plumped; balloon arch fully inflated. No people.
Do · neutral nest
White-on-bone palette. Negative space carries the frame. The eye finds the ball pit before anything else.
Do · color pop in context
Pink, butter, and sage in seasonal proportion; ground stays bone. Color carries the joy without screaming.
Don't · primary-colour clash
Red, blue, yellow at full saturation. Photographs as a kids' party shop. Never our look.
Don't · action as hero
Mid-jump, motion blur, faces in shot. This is a second-tier image; it tells the story but it doesn't sell the booking.
Don't · low light, dark room
Setup before the curtains opened. Soft play looks heavy. No invitation to sit on the floor.
07

Motion & interaction

Subtle, on-scroll, never decorative.

Motion in KiddoKlub is breath, not performance. Hero parallax, reveal-on-scroll for storytelling beats, and full respect for prefers-reduced-motion.

Hero parallax

5% Y travel and 1.04 scale across the first viewport scroll. Driven by Framer Motion useScroll; never CSS-only because the curve must ease.

Reveal-on-scroll

Comparison rows, package cards, theme tiles fade in (8px Y, 0.35s) with a 40ms stagger. once: true; never animate twice.

Reduced-motion

All non-essential motion stops at @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). Hero parallax disables; reveals snap in. Functionality never depends on motion.

09

Theme system

Three themes; locked.

Neutral Nest, White Wonderland, Color Pop. New themes require a strategic reason and Council sign-off; never invented in a marketing brief.

Theme · 01

Neutral Nest

Bone, Sand, dusty Sage. The default look. Reads premium on every villa floor; carries a baby's first birthday and a quiet baby shower without rework.

Props: rattan crates, linen banners, dried pampas, wooden ABC blocks, cream balloon arch, white fencing.

Theme · 02

White Wonderland

Pure white core; subtle pearl, ivory, and silver accents. The "photographs like a magazine" look. Best in light-filled villas with high ceilings.

Props: pearl-finish soft-play, white fencing, white ball pit with cream balls, silver foil number, ivory gauze ceiling drape.

Theme · 03

Color Pop

Dusty Pink, Butter Yellow, Sage; never primary red, blue, yellow. The "joyful but tasteful" look; ideal for second and third birthdays.

Props: pastel ball pit (pink/butter/sage mix), rainbow balloon arch in seasonal palette, gingham picnic cushion, paper rosettes.

11

Bilingual & RTL

EN and AR are equals; never one through the other.

Arabic is written natively, by a native speaker. Layout flips via Next.js layout-level dir="rtl" and CSS logical properties; never via class toggles, which break Framer Motion x-axis animation.

Engineering rules

  • ✓Two routes: / and /ar. Each has its own layout with the right lang and dir.
  • ✓Spacing uses margin-inline-start, padding-inline-end; never margin-left.
  • ✓Numbers, prices, phone numbers stay LTR inside RTL paragraphs (use <bdi> when needed).

Copy rules

  • ✕Never auto-translate. Better to send English than broken Arabic.
  • ✓Arabic uses 29LT Bukra (web fallback IBM Plex Sans Arabic); never Tajawal.
  • ✓Voice in Arabic mirrors voice in English: warm, BLUF, no exclamation marks in body.

12 · The hard list

Don't.

  • No em dash (—). No en dash (–). Use a semicolon or a full stop.
  • No "elevate", "transform", "unleash", "magical", "amazing".
  • No primary-colour palette. No clown red, royal blue, school-bus yellow.
  • No action shot as hero. The hero is the room before the children arrive.
  • No exact Klub Signature price on a public surface. Always "starting from"; quote on WhatsApp.
  • No Equipt branding. No Equipt invoicing. No Equipt logo on a customer surface.
  • No CR number, no LLC, no MOCI; the operation is informal under Nadine personally.
  • No machine-translated Arabic. Never.
  • No Tajawal. Use 29LT Bukra (or Plex Arabic on web).
  • No multi-emoji strings. One quiet emoji, only at the end of a WhatsApp.
  • No invented theme. Three themes only: Neutral Nest, White Wonderland, Color Pop.
  • No mascot, balloon-shaped logo, or hand-lettered font pretending to be playful.
