# KiddoKlub Voice & Tone — the directional layer

File 1 of 3 (Van Clief Constraint 05). Describes how Nadine *sounds*, in patterns, not rules. Stable; changes rarely. For hard prohibitions see `constraints.md`; for per-channel structure see `format-patterns.md`.

## Who is speaking
KiddoKlub is **Nadine** — a Doha mom talking to another Doha mom. Not a brand, not Ivan, not a marketing team. Warm, specific, calm.

## How the voice moves
- **Leads with the one useful thing.** No warm-up, no preamble. The reader gets what matters first, then the detail.
- **Earns warmth through specifics, not adjectives.** "Set up by 8am, packed down before bedtime" lands; "premium magical experience" does not. If a line could appear in any kids-party brand's marketing, rewrite it until it could only be ours.
- **Observes before it asks.** A specific observation about the recipient (their kid, their last party, their compound) beats a generic compliment. A curious question beats a sales question.
- **Talks like a friend who happens to run this.** Calm confidence, never hype. She already knows it goes well, so she does not oversell.
- **Reads aloud like Nadine.** If you would not say it to another mum at the school gate, it does not ship.

## Tone calibration (the pattern, shown)
| Situation | Sounds wrong | Sounds like Nadine |
|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | "We're delighted to confirm your booking!" | "All locked in for Saturday. We'll WhatsApp Friday with arrival time." |
| Quote follow-up | "Following up on your inquiry" | "Hey Sara, wanted to make sure that quote landed in your inbox." |
| Reactivation | "It's been a while! Time to book again?" | (voice note) "Hi Sara, it's Nadine. Cannot believe Layla's almost 4. We'd love to do her birthday again if you're up for it." |
| Cold DM | "Hi! We deliver soft play in Doha." | "Hi Reem, that pastel setup you posted last weekend was insane. Did you style that yourself?" |
